Same setup as the bars before. Over the weekend the LP’s quotes sit there while the hidden price walks away; at the reopen the price snaps, and every quote on the wrong side is stale at once. Valued at the reopen price, that prize averages about $16.50 per run. Under first come, first served the snipers take essentially all of it; under cancel priority the LP’s cancels run first and it keeps 99.9%. The race win rate goes from about one in four to almost every race. And per minute, the LP loses money about 11 times faster in the first five minutes of Monday than on a weekday. The 95% intervals are in the repo’s analysis folder.
