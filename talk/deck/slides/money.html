Each rule sends the prize somewhere else

Bar length = dollars the LP loses to snipers in one weekday hour · 250 ms blocks, 3 snipers twice as fast, mean of 200 runs, same seeds for every rule · analysis/out/headline_b250.csv. Demo: the Profits page splits one run the same way.

Same scale for all three bars: the length is what the LP loses to snipers in one weekday hour, and the colours show where it ends up. First come, first served: speed decides, the LP wins about a third of the races, and the snipers keep most of the $19, the venue taking its 1 bp taker fee on their fills. Priority gas auction: a fee decides, the LP doesn’t bid, so it wins almost nothing and loses twice as much, $40. The snipers bid away nearly all their profit: $14 goes to the chain as priority fees and $26 to the venue as taker fees on their many small fills; they keep 39 cents. Cancel priority: cancels run first in each block, the LP wins 98% of races and loses $3.82, five times less than first come, first served.
