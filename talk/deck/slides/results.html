Results · what the LP loses to snipers, in dollars

Cancel priority is the fix

Five minutes of Monday cost the LP about as much as a whole weekday hour.

One LP, 3 snipers twice as fast, 250 ms blocks · mean of 200 runs per bar, same seeds for every rule · analysis/out/headline_b250.csv. The demo’s Profits page shows one such run live.

Read it as: how many dollars the one LP hands to snipers. Top group: a weekday hour. Bottom group: only the first five minutes after the Monday reopen, on the same dollar scale, so you can see that five minutes of Monday cost about as much as a whole weekday hour. Each bar is the mean of 200 runs with the same seeds for every rule; 95% intervals are within about 10% and in the repo. Cancel priority cuts the LP’s loss about five times on a weekday and about sixty times at the Monday open. PGA is the worst for the LP: the next slides show why and where that money goes. In bps of volume, the unit in the repo’s chart: weekday 0.32, 0.50, 0.07; Monday 3.64, 2.96, 0.07. The dollar amounts depend on how much the made-up regular trader trades; the ratios between rules are what carry over. Across block lengths: at B = 0 the rules are identical by construction; from 50 ms up, cancel priority cuts the loss; at 1-second blocks it weakens, because it protects quotes that were already resting, not ones posted inside the same block.
