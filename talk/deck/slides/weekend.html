The problem

NASDAQ closes. The on-chain book never does.

Tokenized stocks trade around the clock, but their price still comes from Nasdaq. All weekend the book trades without seeing it, and at the reopen it snaps to the real price.

The eclipse is the hidden Nasdaq price. On weekdays every on-chain quote is a bet that you’ll update faster than someone else notices the price moved. On weekends nobody can see the price at all, so Monday’s reopen reprices a whole weekend of quotes in one jump. Robinhood’s 24 Hour Market opens Sunday 8 PM ET; real reopens are a cascade, we model one jump.
