Share of races the LP wins, first come first served, LP as fast as the snipers · 8 seeds × 1 simulated hour · about 11,500 races per row

This is the classic result, and our engine reproduces it within a few tenths of a percent. The point: speed is not the fix. One LP has one cancel; N snipers each have a shot at the stale quote. Even at equal speed the LP loses N out of N+1 races, and it prices that loss into a wider spread that every regular trader pays.
